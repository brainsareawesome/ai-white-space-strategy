# Prompt Log

Three progressive prompts, run in sequence. Each builds on the previous output.

---

## Stage 1: Category & Competitive Scan

**Prompt:**
> Provide a detailed competitive landscape analysis for the Packaged Beverages market in India, focusing specifically on Paper Boat, Tropicana, and Real. For each competitor, outline: target audience, core value proposition, pricing strategy, and public sentiment.

**AI output (summary):** Profiles for all 3 brands covering demographics, value propositions, pricing bands and high-level criticisms.

**Annotation:**
- Worked: captured high-level competitor pillars.
- Failed: over-indexed on brand marketing claims rather than real consumer perceptions.

---

## Stage 2: Perceptual Axis Identification

**Prompt:**
> Based on the competitor analysis, what are 4 different sets of 2x2 variables (axes) that Indian consumers actually use to differentiate brands in this category? Ensure axes represent genuine customer-relevant evidence.

**AI output (summary):** Four axis pairs:
1. Flavor Heritage vs Health Claim
2. Consumption Motivation vs Pack Format
3. Processing Tech vs Price
4. Brand Narrative vs Usage Occasion

**Annotation:**
- Worked: Set 1 (Flavor Profile vs Formulation/Health Claim) gave the sharpest trade-off for purchase behavior.
- Iteration: selected Set 1 for coordinate mapping.

---

## Stage 3: White-Space Detection & Concept

**Prompt:**
> Plot Paper Boat, Tropicana, and Real on a coordinate plane using Set 1 axes. Explain strategic rationale for placements and identify an unoccupied white-space opportunity.

**AI output (summary):** Mapped competitors on a 2x2 grid and identified the top-right quadrant ("Clean-Label Ethnic": zero added sugar plus traditional Indian flavors).

**Annotation:**
- Worked: exposed the core paradox. Paper Boat has ethnic taste but high sugar; Tropicana has zero sugar but Western flavors.
- Needed correction: Tropicana was placed high on "Clean Label" based on its "100% Pure" marketing claim. Consumer reviews showed it is perceived as sweet, processed concentrate, so it was moved down and the Y-axis was redefined as *Consumer-Perceived Sugar & Processing Transparency*.
